Delivery Information & FAQs

Service areas: Ponsonby, Grey Lynn, Parnell, Newmarket, Mount Eden, Epsom, Remuera, and Central Auckland.

Same-day delivery may be available for some orders placed early in the day, but it cannot be guaranteed. At least one working day's notice is recommended.

The website is intended for browsing products and services and submitting enquiries. Online payment is not currently provided.

Customers are encouraged to enquire at least three weeks before smaller events and considerably earlier for weddings or large events.
